# Wallet Mantra -- Overview Page Review (Final)

## Overall Direction

Wallet Mantra has evolved beyond an expense tracker into a financial
companion. The Overview page should answer **"How is my money doing this
month?"** rather than simply displaying numbers.

## Final Information Architecture

1.  Top KPIs (Remaining, Income, Bills Paid)
2.  June in One Sentence
3.  Monthly Breakdown
4.  Spend by Category
5.  Insight
6.  Peace of Mind
7.  Spending Signals
8.  Upcoming Reality
9.  Money Moments
10. What Changed
11. Financial Pulse
12. Tiny Win

## Key Decisions

### Header

-   Keep current header layout.

### KPI Cards

-   Keep only Remaining, Income and Bills Paid.
-   Rename Fixed Paid → Bills Paid.

### Remove Financial Snapshot

-   Remove entirely.
-   It duplicated the KPI values.

### June in One Sentence

-   AI-generated monthly summary.
-   Storytelling instead of numbers.

### Monthly Breakdown

-   Keep stacked progress bar.
-   Consider segment labels instead of only percentages.

### Spend by Category

-   Keep doughnut chart.
-   Keep category filters.
-   Rename Category Winner → Top Spending Category.

### Insight

-   One AI-generated observation per month.

### Peace of Mind

-   Keep circular score.
-   Show score contributors.
-   Expandable 'Why this score?'.

### Spending Signals

-   Replace percentages like 168% with '68% over budget'.

### Upcoming Reality

-   Always visible.
-   If no bills remain, show 'All bills paid this month' and expected
    month-end balance.

### Money Moments

-   Rename Top Spends → Money Moments.
-   Highlight meaningful purchases.

### What Changed

-   Month-over-month trends.
-   For first month show 'Track one more month to unlock trends.'

### Financial Pulse

-   Four pillars:
    -   Stability
    -   Lifestyle
    -   Savings
    -   Consistency

### Tiny Win

-   Celebrate small financial achievements.

## Desktop Improvements

-   Reduce excess whitespace.
-   Use icons alongside section headings.

## Mobile Vision

Design mobile-first with a single-column storytelling flow.

## Product Vision

Wallet Mantra is a financial companion that helps users understand: -
Where money went. - How the month unfolded. - What deserves attention. -
What went well. - What comes next.
