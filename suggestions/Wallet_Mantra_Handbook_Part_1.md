# Wallet Mantra Product Design & Experience Handbook -- Part 1

## Executive Summary

Wallet Mantra is envisioned as an AI-first personal money companion that
helps users understand, improve, and confidently manage their financial
lives. Rather than acting only as a budgeting application, the
experience focuses on proactive guidance, behavioral coaching, and
contextual recommendations.

This handbook documents the product vision, design philosophy, UX
principles, and information architecture that should guide every
feature, interaction, and visual decision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

Build the most trusted AI-powered financial companion that transforms
complex financial decisions into simple daily actions.

## Product Goals

-   Make financial health understandable.
-   Deliver proactive insights instead of passive dashboards.
-   Create trust through transparency.
-   Reduce financial anxiety.
-   Encourage long-term wealth building.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Philosophy

Wallet Mantra follows these principles:

1.  AI should guide, not overwhelm.
2.  Every screen should answer "What should I do next?"
3.  Celebrate progress more than spending.
4.  Use conversational language.
5.  Prioritize clarity over feature density.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# UX Principles

## Human First

Explain financial concepts in plain language.

## Action Oriented

Every insight should end with a recommended action.

## Calm Design

Avoid visual clutter. Use whitespace generously.

## Progressive Disclosure

Reveal advanced analytics only when needed.

## Trust

Always explain why AI generated a recommendation.

# Information Architecture

## Primary Navigation

-   Overview
-   Today
-   Transactions
-   Budgets
-   Goals
-   Investments
-   AI Companion (Tara)
-   Profile & Settings

## Overview Screen

Provides financial health at a glance:

-   Net worth
-   Cash flow
-   Upcoming bills
-   Goal progress
-   AI summary
-   Recommended actions

## Today Screen

Daily financial briefing including:

-   Today's spending
-   Upcoming payments
-   AI coaching
-   Smart reminders
-   Suggested next steps
