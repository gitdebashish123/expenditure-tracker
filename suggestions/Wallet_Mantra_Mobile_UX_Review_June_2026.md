# Wallet Mantra -- Mobile UX Review & Recommendations (June 2026)

## Executive Summary

Wallet Mantra has successfully evolved beyond a traditional expense
tracker into a **financial reflection product**. Instead of only
displaying numbers, it explains what happened, why it matters, and what
deserves the user's attention.

**Overall Rating:** **9.2 / 10**

The next stage of improvement is not about adding more widgets---it is
about simplifying the experience, strengthening visual hierarchy, and
making insights feel even more personal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Key Strengths

## Premium Visual Design

-   Modern dark theme with excellent typography.
-   Colour system is consistent and meaningful.
-   Feels comparable to premium finance products.

## Story-driven Experience

The Overview page now guides the user through: 1. Month summary 2.
Spending breakdown 3. Insights 4. Peace of Mind 5. Spending Signals 6.
Upcoming Reality 7. Money Moments 8. Financial Pulse 9. Tiny Win

Rather than showing isolated metrics, the app tells a complete financial
story.

## Peace of Mind

This is Wallet Mantra's strongest differentiator.

Instead of forcing users to interpret charts, it immediately answers: \>
**"Am I doing okay financially?"**

This should become one of the product's hero features.

## Spending Signals

The redesign is a major improvement. These now feel like intelligent
financial alerts rather than budget progress cards.

## Tiny Win

A brilliant behavioural design decision. Celebrating progress encourages
long-term engagement instead of making users feel guilty.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Improvement Opportunities

## Reduce Overall Scroll Length

The mobile Overview currently spans approximately 8--9 screens.

Recommendation: - Target 4--5 screens. - Merge related sections. -
Collapse secondary content. - Keep advanced information optional.

## Improve Visual Hierarchy

Almost every section is presented inside a similar card.

Recommendation:

Highest priority: - KPI Cards - June in One Sentence - Peace of Mind

Medium priority: - Monthly Breakdown - Spend by Category - Spending
Signals

Lower priority: - Financial Pulse - Money Moments - Tiny Win

Users should naturally know what deserves attention first.

## Financial Pulse

Current labels are attractive but generic.

Instead of: - Stability - Lifestyle - Savings - Consistency

Use personalised insights such as: - Grocery spending increased 32%. -
Savings exceeded your 6-month average. - Tracked expenses 28 consecutive
days.

Personalisation makes the product feel significantly smarter.

## June in One Sentence

Excellent concept.

Keep the summary under two lines whenever possible and focus only on: -
Bills - Savings - Remaining balance

## Money Moments

Great idea but currently resembles another transaction list.

Instead, tell memorable stories: - Biggest Purchase - Best Investment -
Largest Gift - Learning Investment

This makes the section more memorable.

## Header

The mobile header consumes valuable vertical space.

Compress into a single row: Wallet Mantra \| Month ▼ \| Theme \| Profile

## Bottom Navigation

Looks clean.

Consider making the active tab slightly more prominent using weight or
icon size.

## KPI Cards

Removing the duplicate Financial Snapshot cards was the correct UX
decision.

Avoid repeating the same numbers elsewhere.

## Adaptive Insights

Replacing "What Changed?" with adaptive insights is the right long-term
direction.

It works equally well for: - New users - Users with missing historical
data - Inconsistent trackers - Power users

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Recommended Information Architecture

## Layer 1 -- Snapshot

Purpose: **How am I doing?** - KPI Cards - June in One Sentence - Peace
of Mind

## Layer 2 -- Understand

Purpose: **Why?** - Monthly Breakdown - Spend by Category - Spending
Signals - Insight Card

## Layer 3 -- Reflect & Improve

Purpose: **What's next?** - Upcoming Reality - Money Moments - Financial
Pulse - Tiny Win

This creates a more natural flow and reduces cognitive load.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Verdict

Wallet Mantra no longer feels like an expense tracker.

It feels like a thoughtful financial companion that interprets spending
behaviour instead of merely displaying transactions.

The next improvements should focus on: - Reducing scroll length -
Strengthening information hierarchy - Increasing personalisation -
Continuing to simplify

**Final UX Score: 9.2 / 10**

The product is approaching launch quality. Future gains will come from
thoughtful refinement rather than adding more features.
