# Wallet Mantra Login Page Design Guide

## Goal

Create a premium fintech login experience that balances marketing
content on the left with authentication on the right.

## Layout

-   Full viewport height (`100vh`)
-   Two-column layout
    -   **Left:** 55%
    -   **Right:** 45%
-   Max content width: `1600px`
-   Horizontal padding: `48–64px`

## Background

-   Base: `#0B0B10`
-   Subtle radial gradients in navy and gold
-   Very low-opacity particle/grid pattern (5--8%)

## Left Hero

### Header

-   Wallet Mantra logo
-   Tagline: **Know. Plan. Grow.**

### Headline

**Your AI Financial Companion**

### Feature list

-   AI-powered expense tracking
-   Smart budgeting & insights
-   Savings goals & reminders
-   Investment tracking
-   Secure & private

Each item uses a rounded square icon with gold outline.

### Floating insight cards

-   Saved this month
-   Budget score
-   Expense forecast

### Product preview

Show a tilted desktop dashboard mockup with: - Charts - Recent
transactions - AI insight card - Sidebar navigation

### Social proof

Stats row: - 12,000+ Active Users - ₹4.2 Cr+ Expenses Tracked - 4.8/5
Rating

Below that: - Customer testimonial - Small avatar

## Visual Style

### Colors

-   Background: #0B0B10
-   Card: #12121A
-   Primary: #6A5CFF
-   Gold: #D9B34A
-   Green: #35D39A
-   Text: #F5F5F7
-   Secondary text: #9B9BA8

### Typography

-   Headings: 48--56px
-   Card title: 28--36px
-   Body: 16--18px
-   Small labels: 13--14px

### Buttons

-   Height: 56px
-   Radius: 14px
-   Purple gradient
-   Soft hover glow

### Inputs

-   Height: 56px
-   Radius: 14px
-   Dark fill with gold focus border

## Motion

-   Floating cards: slow 4--6s animation
-   Dashboard: slight parallax
-   Button hover: lift 2px
-   Inputs: glow on focus
-   Background particles: slow movement

## Responsive

### Desktop (\>1440px)

55/45 layout.

### Laptop (1024--1440px)

Dashboard scales down, card stays \~520px.

### Tablet

Stack vertically.

### Mobile

Login card first, marketing below.

## UX Notes

-   Keep whitespace intentional.
-   Do not overload the hero.
-   Use realistic finance data.
-   Maintain consistent icon style.
-   Keep animations subtle.

## Inspiration

Aim for the polish of premium fintech products while preserving Wallet
Mantra's blue, gold and charcoal identity.
